Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026.

Purpose

Rclone Personal is a private, single-user configuration of rclone. It accesses the owner's Google Drive only with the owner's authorization to list, read, copy, update, or delete files as requested by the owner.

Data handling

Files and metadata are processed on the owner's computer and transferred to or from cloud storage selected by the owner. This website does not receive Google Drive files or OAuth tokens.

Storage and sharing

OAuth credentials are stored in the owner's local rclone configuration. Data is not sold or used for advertising. Files are transferred only to destinations configured by the owner.

Control and revocation

The owner can stop using the application, remove its local configuration, and revoke its access through Google Account security settings.

Contact

For questions, use the developer contact email shown on the application's Google OAuth consent screen.
